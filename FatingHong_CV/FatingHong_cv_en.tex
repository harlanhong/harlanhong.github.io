\begin{rSection}{Introduction}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=10pt]
\setlength{\itemsep}{-5pt}
I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), working with Prof. Peter Wonka. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) under the supervision of Prof. Dan Xu, and my M.Sc. from Sun Yat-sen University under the supervision of Prof. Wei-Shi Zheng. My research centers on \textbf{human-centric video}: generating, animating, and understanding people in video, from talking heads and audio-driven portraits to free-viewpoint full-body animation and 4D avatars, as well as the human motion that drives them. My work has appeared in CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, IEEE TPAMI, and IJCV, and I have conducted research at Meta Reality Labs, Adobe Research, and Tencent Hunyuan.

\end{itemize}
