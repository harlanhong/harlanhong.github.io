Biography

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), working with Prof. Peter Wonka. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Prof. DAN XU. I received my master's degree in 2021 at Sun Yat-sen University, where I was advised by Prof. Wei-Shi Zheng and researched in video understanding.
